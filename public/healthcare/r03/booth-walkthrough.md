# Generic Post-Training Opener: R09

Staff narration only; this document is not linked from the page.

| What to say | What to show |
| --- | --- |
| An open model gives you a capable starting point. Post-training helps adapt its behavior to the tasks you care about. | Start Nemotron in the original gym. |
| Completing a rep is not enough; technique matters. | Watch the first attempt: the weights lift, but the body swings. |
| NeMo Gym provides the practice environment and evaluation. NeMo RL turns feedback into model updates. | Let the feedback and model-update beats play. The robot has not grown yet. |
| Then it practices the improved technique, repeatedly. | Watch two complete controlled reps. Let both finish. |
| The practice is paying off. | After a short pause, shoulders and arms fill out, highlighted by a brief green halo. |
| We need varied tasks, not just one exercise. | Repeat at bench press and elliptical. Smooth abdominal contours appear after bench practice, then deepen after elliptical practice; a brief area halo highlights the gain. |
| The improvement should hold, rep after rep. | Select See the change for three controlled reps with the completed physique, followed by the final hold. |

The silent loop lasts 73 seconds. Play it from the beginning to review the timing;
use workflow steps or station buttons for a shorter live presentation. Muscle
growth happens after the practice reps, never during the model-update beat or
while a rep is in progress. Gains persist until replay or the next loop.

Completion plus technique remains the reward criterion. The circuit is a scripted
analogy, not a live Nemotron/GRPO run, measured performance result or specification
of optimizer batching. Coaches symbolize evaluation. Muscles and softly sculpted abs symbolize improved
capability in the same model, not extra parameters or hardware. Held-out evaluation
is still needed to establish generalization in an actual use case.
